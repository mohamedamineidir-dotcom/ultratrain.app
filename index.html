How it works

Three steps to the start line.

01

Answer Ultra's questions

One question per screen, no form. Where you run, which ultras tempt you, your best times, how many days a week you can train. "Not sure" is always an answer.

02

Get your plan and your rank

Your answers place you on the ladder. Pick a race, or a first ultra, or just a fitness block, and the plan is built from race day backwards.

03

Train and climb

Every run and every completed session earns XP. Validated rest days too. Finish a race and your level is recomputed. Stop for weeks and the rank slips, like real life.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    How much does UltraTrain cost?

    UltraTrain is a subscription bought in the app: 14.99 € per month or 49.99 € per year. There is no free tier: the whole app is unlocked from day one, and you can cancel anytime from your App Store subscriptions.

    Which watches and apps sync with it?

    UltraTrain has its own Apple Watch app and reads your runs from Apple Health and Strava. Garmin, COROS, Suunto and Polar runs come through Strava or Apple Health. Direct Garmin and COROS sync is not available yet. Completed runs can be uploaded to Strava too.

    I have never run an ultra. Is it for me?

    Yes. The My first ultra path builds you up from your current base to a 50K, 60K, 80K or 100K, with safe minimum timelines. The rank starts you where you really are, and finishing your first ultra is what unlocks Diamond. You can also pick Just build fitness with no race at all.

    Road, trail or backyard?

    All three. Plans cover 5K to marathon on road or trail, ultras of any distance and elevation, and backyard and elimination-laps formats where the pace is imposed and the goal is counted in loops. Elevation always counts: every 100 m of climb adds one kilometre of effort.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes. Cancel from Settings, your name, Subscriptions on your iPhone. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Your data stays in your private iCloud until you delete it.
